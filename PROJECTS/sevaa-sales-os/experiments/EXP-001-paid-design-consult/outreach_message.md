## Rules for sending

- Send one-to-one only, never a broadcast list or a status post.
- Personalise the first line with the real reason you thought of them; if you cannot name one, they do not fit the buyer and should not be messaged.
- Do not include any link until they say yes; then send the payment link from PAYMENT_LINK.md in a separate message.
- Log every send in contacts.csv by code (C01, C02, …) the same day, with the date in sent_at.
- Reply to every answer within 24 hours and record reply (interested / declined) and reason_code (price / timing / no_need / other) in contacts.csv.
