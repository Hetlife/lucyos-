# Call checklist — Design Clarity Call (90 min)

_Every section of brief_template.md is answerable from these questions. Tick as you go._

## 0-10 min: context

- [ ] What made you want to look at this now, and what happens if nothing changes?
- [ ] Who lives or works in the space, and who decides on the design and the money?
- [ ] Have you worked with an architect or interior designer before, and how did it go?

## 10-40 min: the property and the goal

- [ ] Property type and carpet area in square feet (ask them to read it from the agreement if unsure)?
- [ ] Possession date or current state of the site, and is any work already done?
- [ ] Which rooms matter most to you and why?
- [ ] What must the finished space let you do that it does not today?
- [ ] Is there an existing plan or drawing you can share after the call?

## 40-65 min: constraints and budget

- [ ] What is fixed and cannot move (structure, plumbing lines, society rules, vastu preferences)?
- [ ] What budget range do you have in mind for everything, and what is it based on?
- [ ] Which matters more if they conflict: finishing sooner, spending less, or getting exactly the look?
- [ ] Will the work happen while the space is in use?

## 65-85 min: direction and reactions

- [ ] What have you seen recently that you liked, and what did you dislike?
- [ ] Reaction to the one direction I am proposing, and to the alternative I am rejecting?
- [ ] Which of the three next steps can you do this week?

## 85-90 min: close

- [ ] Tell them the brief arrives within 48 hours and how the INR 2,499 is credited
- [ ] Confirm the code you will use for them in contacts.csv and that no name goes into the system
